Sending interest does not open a conversation by itself. A chat is created only after both members express interest. Shake & Chat starts anonymously: names, photos and profile details stay hidden unless both members consent to reveal them. A member may withdraw their consent until the other member also agrees; after mutual consent, profile details are revealed for that conversation. The existing 72-hour conversation and safety rules apply. Do not use the feature to stalk, harass or attempt to identify another member's precise location.
