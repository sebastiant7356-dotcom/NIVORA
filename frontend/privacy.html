Privacy Notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

BEYOND is a social discovery service for adults aged 18 and over. This notice describes the current minimal-data implementation. The operator and privacy contact is nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com.

1. Account information that is retained

BEYOND uses Google sign-in through Supabase Auth. The authentication provider retains the account identifier and the basic provider/account information required to authenticate the account, which may include an email address and provider metadata. BEYOND does not ask for, receive, or store your Google password. Your Google email address is not shown to other members.

2. Information processed temporarily

To provide the service, the backend may temporarily process profile details you enter, your approximate discovery location and selected radius, mutual-interest state, chat text, anonymous post text, and friend requests, and blocks. These items are held in volatile server memory only, with a maximum inactivity lifetime of 12 hours. Safety reports and account appeals are the exception: the relevant account ID, report or appeal reason, short optional description, status and resolution are stored securely for moderation for up to 90 days. Moderator action records are retained for up to 365 days, and an active account suspension or ban record remains until lifted or the account is deleted. No ordinary chat history, profile, location or post content is retained. A server restart, session expiry, logout-related cleanup, or memory eviction may erase them earlier. They are not a recoverable history.

Exact coordinates are used only for distance calculations while discovery is active. The application returns approximate distance, not your coordinates or address. Turn off discovery to remove the active location from the current server process.

3. Photos, voice, payments and history

Profile-photo uploads and voice-message uploads are disabled. BEYOND does not provide persistent chat history, persistent friend lists, saved profiles, saved posts, or paid features in this version. Text messages and anonymous posts exist only temporarily in server memory. Do not send highly sensitive information in a chat.

4. Safety and moderation

Blocks remain temporary and may be lost when the server restarts or their inactivity lifetime expires. Reports and account appeals are stored as limited safety records for up to 90 days so authorised moderators can review them. Moderator action records are retained for up to 365 days. Account suspension and ban status is retained until lifted or the account is deleted. Reports may contain the reporter and reported account identifiers, a reason, and a short optional description; ordinary chat messages, photos, profile details and location history are not copied into the report database. Access is restricted to authorised staff and moderator actions are audit-logged. This does not guarantee an immediate response or emergency assistance. Contact local emergency services if you are in immediate danger. Report urgent safety concerns to nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com.

5. Technical processing and providers

Google provides sign-in, Supabase provides authentication, and Render hosts the backend. Authentication requests and temporary application data are transmitted over encrypted connections. Operational request logs are limited to request method, route, response status and duration; message bodies, passwords, access tokens and coordinates must not be logged. Providers may process data in regions outside your country under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Existing legacy records

BEYOND previously used database tables and file storage for profiles and social content. This code version stops new member-content writes, but previously created database rows or stored files are not automatically removed by deploying it. Legacy records must be reviewed and deleted through a separately verified cleanup operation. Until that cleanup is completed and verified, this notice must not be interpreted as confirmation that all historical content has already been erased.

7. Account deletion and requests

You can request deletion of your Google-authenticated BEYOND account from the application. Account deletion removes the Supabase Auth user when the deletion request succeeds. Because temporary content is not a durable archive, it cannot be exported after it has expired or been erased. For privacy questions or data requests, contact nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com.

8. Changes

This notice must be updated before any feature begins storing ordinary member content, uploading media, processing payments, or changing safety-record retention. Do not use a version of the application whose actual behaviour materially differs from this notice.
