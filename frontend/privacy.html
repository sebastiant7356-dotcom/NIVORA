Privacy Notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

NIVORA is a Kerala-only social discovery app for adults aged 21–40, built around Shake & Chat, anonymous posts and optional friendships. This notice explains the personal information handled by the service and the choices available to members. The current individual operator is Sebastian Thomas. For privacy and grievance enquiries, contact nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com. NIVORA is operated by an individual and is not a registered company. The grievance contact is the operator, Sebastian Thomas.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: Google account identifier and basic account information supplied through Google authentication, such as name and email address. Your email address is not displayed to other members.
  • Profile: name, age, Kerala district, work or study, biography and selected interests. You control whether your profile is visible to other members.
  • Photos: up to four profile images you choose to upload (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each).
  • Optional Shake & Chat location: if you enable discovery and grant location permission, NIVORA stores your coordinates securely and uses the radius you select (1–100 km) to find nearby members. Other members see only an approximate distance, never your coordinates or address. Location is retained while discovery is enabled and deleted when you turn it off or delete your account.
  • Chats and friendships: Shake connections, text messages, voice recordings you choose to send, friend requests and accepted friendships. Voice messages are limited to 60 seconds and 2 MB each. Shake chats do not have an automatic time limit; either member can end or block the conversation.
  • Anonymous posts: post text, audience choice (all NIVORA members in Kerala or a selected nearby radius), post time and moderation status. Your identity is not shown to other members on an anonymous post; authorised staff may access the author record when investigating a report.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, moderation decisions and security/audit records.
  • Technical data: basic service request and error information needed to operate and secure the service. We do not intentionally log authentication tokens, message bodies or phone numbers in server request logs.

NIVORA uses Google sign-in only. It does not use phone-number or SMS OTP sign-in. At launch, matching and messaging are free and NIVORA does not collect payment details or process member payments.

2. How we use information

When you separately opt in to Shake & Chat, we use your device location only to find members within the radius you select (1–100 km). You can change the radius or disable location sharing at any time from the Shake tab. Turning it off deletes the stored location. The feature does not publish your exact location or address.

We use the information to manage accounts, show nearby discovery results within your selected radius, provide chat and voice messages, manage mutual friend requests, publish anonymous posts to the audience you select, enforce profile visibility choices, prevent contact between blocked members, respond to reports, protect the service and provide data export or account deletion.

3. Profile approval and safety review

New customer profiles are activated automatically. Uploaded profile photos remain pending until a staff member approves or rejects each photo; only approved photos are shown to other members. This photo check does not verify a person's identity, age or background. Staff account investigations are limited to accounts with member reports. Authorised Admin and Moderator staff may inspect relevant profile information and, when necessary to investigate a report, the conversation's text or voice messages. Access to reported conversations is recorded in an audit log.

NIVORA does not currently run routine AI screening or send member profile text to an external AI provider. Staff review is intended to focus on accounts with member reports or other documented safety concerns. Authorised staff access is role-limited and recorded in an audit log. Relevant report evidence may remain accessible to authorised staff where needed to handle, review or document a safety complaint.

4. Who processes information

The service uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, and Render for application hosting. NIVORA does not currently use an external AI provider to screen member profiles. If this changes, the notice and any required consent will be updated before that processing begins. Current deployment locations are Supabase database/storage in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2), and Render application hosting in Singapore. Google authentication may involve processing in other regions. This means member information may be processed outside India. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy notices; the operator must have Indian counsel verify applicable cross-border transfer requirements and provider contracts before launch.

We do not sell member personal information. In Shake discovery, members may see limited profile details and approximate distance. Chat starts anonymously: names, photos and profile details remain hidden until both members separately consent to reveal them. Your profile visibility setting can keep profile details hidden. An anonymous post does not display your profile name or profile link to other members. Author identity remains available to authorised staff for safety investigations. Members never receive your Google email address or authentication credentials.

5. Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from My space. When deletion succeeds, NIVORA deletes your customer profile, profile-photo files, match conversations, text messages, voice-message files and related match data, subject to specific preservation duties described below. Staff accounts cannot self-delete through this customer flow.

180-day registration retention: After account cancellation, NIVORA retains a restricted record containing only the account's Google registration identifier, Google email address (if supplied), registration display name (if supplied), sign-in provider and original registration timestamp for 180 days. This meets the applicable intermediary registration-information retention requirement. It is stored separately from customer profiles, is not used for matching and is not shown to other members. A scheduled daily database job permanently purges these records after the 180-day period.

Other limited records: Reports, complaint evidence, moderation records and security/audit logs may be retained for the period required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect members, enforce these terms or respond to lawful requests. Where NIVORA is required to remove or disable access to information in response to a qualifying legal notice or complaint, it will preserve that information and associated records for 180 days, or longer where lawfully required, without compromising evidence. Payments are not collected for the free launch. Backup copies may persist for a limited period under provider backup cycles.

The 180-day registration record is a limited legal-retention exception; it does not mean all active account data is retained for 180 days.

6. Your choices

You can edit your profile, choose public or hidden profile visibility, choose a Shake radius, turn location sharing off, post or delete your anonymous posts, accept or decline friend requests, block or report another member, download available account data and request account deletion. Do not include sensitive information in your profile or messages that you do not want a match or authorised staff reviewing a report to see.

7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated API routes and private storage with time-limited signed links for profile photos and voice messages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure and do not share sign-in codes or credentials.

8. Adults only and safety

NIVORA is intended only for people aged 21–40. Do not create an account for a minor or misrepresent your age. If you believe a member is underage or acting abusively, use Report and stop communicating. NIVORA does not conduct independent identity or background checks and cannot guarantee another member's statements or conduct.

9. Contact and grievances

Operator and grievance contact: Sebastian Thomas, individual operator. Email: nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com. NIVORA does not currently publish a phone number or postal address.

Send complaints by email with the subject “NIVORA Grievance”, a description of the issue and relevant profile or match reference. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and ordinarily resolved within 15 days. Specified content complaints are handled within shorter statutory timelines where applicable. You may use the appeal route available under applicable Indian rules if dissatisfied with a grievance decision.

10. Changes

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date should be shown here. Material changes should be communicated to members and, where required, fresh consent should be collected.
