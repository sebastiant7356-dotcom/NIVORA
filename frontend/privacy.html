Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

NIVORA is a Kerala-focused introductions and matchmaking service for adults aged 21–40. This notice explains the personal information handled by the service and the choices available to members. 1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: Google account identifier and basic account information supplied through Google authentication, such as name and email address. Your email address is not displayed to other members.
  • Profile: name, age, district/location, gender, work or study, biography, partner preferences, relationship intention, preferred age range and selected values.
  • Photos: up to four profile images you choose to upload (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each).
  • Matching and conversations: match records, daily matching usage, text messages and voice recordings you choose to send. Voice messages are limited to 60 seconds and 2 MB each. Conversations have a 72-hour active window.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, moderation decisions and security/audit records.
  • Technical data: basic service request and error information needed to operate and secure the service. We do not intentionally log authentication tokens, message bodies or phone numbers in server request logs.

2. How we use information

We use the information to create and manage accounts, calculate compatible introductions, enforce the limit of up to five matching recommendations per member per Kerala calendar day, provide text and voice messaging, prevent repeated matches between blocked members, respond to reports, protect the service and provide data export or account deletion.

3. Profile approval and safety review

New customer profiles and uploaded profile photos are activated automatically; this does not mean a person's identity, age, background or photos have been independently verified. Staff review is intended to focus on accounts reported by members or other safety concerns. Authorised Admin and Moderator staff may inspect relevant profile information and, when necessary to investigate a report, the conversation's text or voice messages. Access to reported conversations is recorded in an audit log.

Optional automated screening may be run by staff as an advisory aid. It does not automatically approve, reject or suspend an account. If external AI review is enabled by the operator, only redacted work/study and biography text is sent to the configured AI provider for an advisory result. The operator must confirm whether this feature is enabled and identify the provider before launch.

4. Who processes information

The service uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, and Render for application hosting. If optional external AI review is enabled, the configured AI provider may also process redacted profile text. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy notices. The operator must verify provider locations, applicable transfer arrangements and current contractual terms before launch.

We do not sell member personal information. Profile information is shown only as needed to present a match. Matched members may see your first name, age, district, work/study, biography, relationship intention, selected values and approved-for-display profile photos. They do not receive your Google email address or authentication credentials.

5. Retention and deletion

Profile, match, text-message, voice-message and photo records are retained while the account and associated records remain in the service. You can request permanent account deletion from My space. The deletion workflow removes the account, profile, associated match conversations, profile-photo files and voice-message files. Reports and audit/security records may be retained, with direct account references detached where the database permits, to investigate abuse, protect members and meet legal obligations. A precise retention period for these retained records must be set by the operator and reviewed before launch.

6. Your choices

You can edit your profile, choose whether to upload photos or send messages, block or report another member, download available account data, and request account deletion. Do not include sensitive information in your profile or messages that you do not want a match or authorised staff reviewing a report to see.

7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated API routes and private storage with time-limited signed links for profile photos and voice messages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure and do not share sign-in codes or credentials.

8. Adults only and safety

NIVORA is intended only for people aged 21–40. Do not create an account for a minor or misrepresent your age. If you believe a member is underage or acting abusively, use Report and stop communicating. NIVORA does not conduct independent identity or background checks and cannot guarantee another member's statements or conduct.
