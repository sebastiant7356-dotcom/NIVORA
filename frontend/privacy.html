Privacy & safety notice

What we collect

Your mobile number (for OTP sign-in, held by our authentication provider), name, age, location, gender, work/study, about-you text, partner preferences, values, consent records, chat messages and call signalling between matched members, reports and safety actions, and payment status (not card details).

How it is used

To verify you, let staff review your profile, find a mutually suitable match, enable private chat and voice for 72 hours, keep members safe, and process the ₹50 payment if enabled.

Who sees what

Matched members see only first name, age, location, work/study, about-you, intention and values. Your phone number and last name are never shown to other members. Staff see profile details and screening notes; opening a reported chat is recorded in an audit log.
