Privacy Notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

NIVORA is a Kerala-focused introductions and matchmaking service for adults aged 21–40. This notice explains the personal information handled by the service and the choices available to members. The current individual operator is Sebastian Thomas. For privacy and grievance enquiries, contact nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com. NIVORA is operated by an individual and is not a registered company. The grievance contact is the operator, Sebastian Thomas.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: Google account identifier and basic account information supplied through Google authentication, such as name and email address. Your email address is not displayed to other members.
  • Profile: name, age, district/location, gender, work or study, biography, partner preferences, relationship intention, preferred age range and selected values.
  • Photos: up to four profile images you choose to upload (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each).
  • Matching and conversations: match records, daily matching usage, text messages and voice recordings you choose to send. Voice messages are limited to 60 seconds and 2 MB each. Conversations have a 72-hour active window.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, moderation decisions and security/audit records.
  • Technical data: basic service request and error information needed to operate and secure the service. We do not intentionally log authentication tokens, message bodies or phone numbers in server request logs.

NIVORA uses Google sign-in only. It does not use phone-number or SMS OTP sign-in. At launch, matching and messaging are free and NIVORA does not collect payment details or process member payments.

2. How we use information

We use the information to create and manage accounts, calculate compatible introductions, enforce the limit of up to five matching recommendations per member per Kerala calendar day, provide text and voice messaging, prevent repeated matches between blocked members, respond to reports, protect the service and provide data export or account deletion.

3. Profile approval and safety review

New customer profiles and uploaded profile photos are activated automatically; this does not mean a person's identity, age, background or photos have been independently verified. Staff review is intended to focus on accounts reported by members or other safety concerns. Authorised Admin and Moderator staff may inspect relevant profile information and, when necessary to investigate a report, the conversation's text or voice messages. Access to reported conversations is recorded in an audit log.

NIVORA does not currently run routine AI screening or send member profile text to an external AI provider. Staff review is intended to focus on accounts with member reports or other documented safety concerns. Authorised staff access is role-limited and recorded in an audit log. Relevant report evidence may remain accessible to authorised staff where needed to handle, review or document a safety complaint.

4. Who processes information

The service uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, and Render for application hosting. NIVORA does not currently use an external AI provider to screen member profiles. If this changes, the notice and any required consent will be updated before that processing begins. Current deployment locations are Supabase database/storage in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2), and Render application hosting in Singapore. Google authentication may involve processing in other regions. This means member information may be processed outside India. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy notices; the operator must have Indian counsel verify applicable cross-border transfer requirements and provider contracts before launch.

We do not sell member personal information. Profile information is shown only as needed to present a match. Matched members may see your first name, age, district, work/study, biography, relationship intention, selected values and approved-for-display profile photos. They do not receive your Google email address or authentication credentials.

5. Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from My space. When deletion succeeds, NIVORA deletes your customer profile, profile-photo files, match conversations, text messages, voice-message files and related match data, subject to specific preservation duties described below. Staff accounts cannot self-delete through this customer flow.

180-day registration retention: After account cancellation, NIVORA retains a restricted record containing only the account's Google registration identifier, Google email address (if supplied), registration display name (if supplied), sign-in provider and original registration timestamp for 180 days. This meets the applicable intermediary registration-information retention requirement. It is stored separately from customer profiles, is not used for matching and is not shown to other members. A scheduled daily database job permanently purges these records after the 180-day period.

Other limited records: Reports, complaint evidence, moderation records and security/audit logs may be retained for the period required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect members, enforce these terms or respond to lawful requests. Information removed or disabled in response to a qualifying legal notice or complaint may be preserved with associated records for at least 180 days, or longer where lawfully required. Payments are not collected for the free launch. Backup copies may persist for a limited period under provider backup cycles.

The 180-day registration record is a limited legal-retention exception; it does not mean all active account data is retained for 180 days.

6. Your choices

You can edit your profile, choose whether to upload photos or send messages, block or report another member, download available account data, and request account deletion. Do not include sensitive information in your profile or messages that you do not want a match or authorised staff reviewing a report to see.

7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated API routes and private storage with time-limited signed links for profile photos and voice messages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure and do not share sign-in codes or credentials.

8. Adults only and safety

NIVORA is intended only for people aged 21–40. Do not create an account for a minor or misrepresent your age. If you believe a member is underage or acting abusively, use Report and stop communicating. NIVORA does not conduct independent identity or background checks and cannot guarantee another member's statements or conduct.

9. Contact and grievances

Operator and grievance contact: Sebastian Thomas, individual operator. Email: nivora.helpdesk@gmail.com. NIVORA does not currently publish a phone number or postal address.

Send complaints by email with the subject “NIVORA Grievance”, a description of the issue and relevant profile or match reference. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and ordinarily resolved within 15 days. Specified content complaints are handled within shorter statutory timelines where applicable. You may use the appeal route available under applicable Indian rules if dissatisfied with a grievance decision.

10. Changes

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date should be shown here. Material changes should be communicated to members and, where required, fresh consent should be collected.
